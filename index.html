The honest version

Everyone wants the house that stops traffic. Almost nobody wants the weekend it costs.

You've pictured it. Warm light tracing every roofline, the yard glowing, neighbors slowing down to look.

Then reality shows up. Which lights actually match your house? How many strands? What size? Do the colors clash with the brick? And who, exactly, is getting on the roof in December?

So you compromise. A few strands along the porch.

Boxes of tangled lights that half work.

An afternoon on a ladder in the cold, followed by another afternoon in January taking it all down.

Then the bins go back in the garage, where they take up space for eleven months and come out worse than they went in.

It doesn't have to be your project.
It can just be your view.

FAQ

Questions people ask before booking

Do I buy the Christmas lights or rent them?
You rent them. All holiday lighting is rental only, which means no upfront product cost and nothing to store. We install everything, maintain it through the season, take it down, and store it until next year. If you want a different look next season, you're not stuck with lights you already bought.
What's actually included in the service?
Everything: custom design, a visual preview of your property, all materials, professional installation, in-season maintenance for installation-related issues, end-of-season takedown, and off-season storage. You don't handle any part of it.
Do I get to see the design before you install it?
Yes, and it's one of the reasons people hire us. We create a design specific to your property and show you a preview of how it will look. You approve it, or ask for changes, before installation day.
Do you decorate the inside of the house too, or just the outside?
Both. Along with the exterior lighting, we install wreaths, garland for mantels, railings and doorframes, and fully decorated Christmas trees. Every piece comes pre-lit, and the interior is designed to match what we do outside, so the whole property reads as one look.
What colors can I choose from?
Three combinations: Gold and Silver, Red and Gold, or Blue and Silver. Each one is put together as a complete set — wreath, garland, and tree — so nothing clashes, and every piece comes with lights already built in. Tell us the room and we'll show you which one we'd pick for it.
Can I control the permanent lighting from my phone?
Yes. Permanent lighting is controlled from an app on your phone over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Warm white for everyday evenings, seasonal colors when you want them, and color changes whenever you feel like it.
You're a newer company. Why should I trust you with my property?
Fair question. The company is newer, but the crew behind it has 10+ years of hands-on lighting experience. We deliver the same standard of design and installation as the largest companies in the area, at fairer pricing, and we back it with in-season support. We're building a reputation right now, which means your project gets more attention, not less.
